Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Quit Munchies (“the app”) is made by Amoriel Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything you log stays on your phone. We never sell or share your data.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple, your data is backed up and synced securely so you can use it on a new phone, and only you can access it.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics in the app.
  • You can back up, restore or delete all your data at any time in Settings › Your data.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following on your device, in the app’s private storage (and, only if you sign in, in your private backup, see below):

  • your pet’s name and, if you choose one, a photo of your pet;
  • your currency, usual order cost, weekly order budget and craving times;
  • what you log: skipped orders, orders, events, amounts, and what you ate instead;
  • your jars, goals and goal labels;
  • your settings, such as theme and sound.

If you don’t sign in, this stays on your phone and is deleted with the app.

Photos

If you choose a photo of your pet (a Munchies Plus feature), the app copies it into its own storage on your phone so it can show it. Only one photo is kept: it’s deleted when you replace or remove it. The photo is never uploaded. The app only sees the photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

Notifications

Check-ins are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Backups

If you use Save a backup, the app creates a file with your data and opens your phone’s share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on) is up to you, and that service’s own privacy terms apply. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Sign in with Apple, backup and sync (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an ID and, only if you allow it, your name and an email address (which can be Apple’s private relay address). There are no passwords.

While you’re signed in, the app backs up your data (the items listed above, except the pet photo, your Plus status and per-phone settings such as sound and theme) to our database so you can restore it on a new phone and use the app on more than one device. Here’s how it’s handled:

  • Where: the database is run by Supabase on our behalf. It is used only to store and return your data to you.
  • Who can see it: only you. Database rules let a signed-in person read and change only their own data. We don’t look at it, and we never sell or share it or use it for advertising.
  • Security: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain.
  • Your ID is also shared with RevenueCat (below) so your Plus purchase follows you to a new phone.

You can sign out in Settings › Account at any time; your backup stays until you delete it. Delete account (Settings › Account) permanently deletes your account and your backup, and revokes the app’s Sign in with Apple access.

Purchases

Munchies Plus is sold through Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active: RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or your Sign in with Apple ID, if you signed in) and your purchase history from the App Store. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect any information from them (or from anyone).

Your rights

You’re always in control of your data: view it in the app, export it with Save a backup, erase it from your phone with Delete all my data, and, if you signed in, delete your account and cloud backup with Delete account. If you have questions or requests, contact us at amorielapps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above, and explain any important change in the app before it applies to you.
