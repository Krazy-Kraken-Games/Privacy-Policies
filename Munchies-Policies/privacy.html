Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Quit Munchies (“the app”) is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Everything you log stays on your phone. We don’t have servers that receive it, and we don’t sell or share it.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple, we only use it to keep your Plus purchase with you.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics in the app.
  • You can back up, restore or delete all your data at any time in Settings › Your data.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • your pet’s name and, if you choose one, a photo of your pet;
  • your currency, usual order cost, weekly order budget and craving times;
  • what you log: skipped orders, orders, events, amounts, and what you ate instead;
  • your jars, goals and goal labels;
  • your settings, such as theme and sound.

We can’t see any of this. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Photos

If you choose a photo of your pet (a Munchies Plus feature), the app copies it into its own storage on your phone so it can show it. Only one photo is kept: it’s deleted when you replace or remove it. The photo is never uploaded. The app only sees the photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

Notifications

Check-ins are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Backups

If you use Save a backup, the app creates a file with your data and opens your phone’s share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on) is up to you, and that service’s own privacy terms apply. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous ID and, only if you allow it, your name and an email address (which can be Apple’s private relay address). These are stored on your phone, and the ID is shared with RevenueCat (below) so your Plus purchase follows you to a new phone. You can sign out in Settings at any time.

Purchases

Munchies Plus is sold through Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active: RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or your Sign in with Apple ID, if you signed in) and your purchase history from the App Store. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect any information from them (or from anyone).

Your rights

Because your data never leaves your phone, you’re always in control of it: you can view it in the app, export it with Save a backup, and erase it with Delete all my data. If you have questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if we add optional sync), we’ll update this page and the date above, and explain the change in the app before it applies to you.
