Quick answers

I bought Plus but it isn’t showing

Open the app, go to Settings › Munchies Plus and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Quit Munchies. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Lifetime Plus never renews, so there’s nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I’m moving to a new phone. How do I keep my jar?

Easiest: Sign in with Apple (Settings › Account) and your jar is backed up automatically. On the new phone, sign in on the welcome screen and everything comes back. Prefer no account? Use Settings › Your data › Save a backup, keep the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive or email it to yourself), then Restore from a backup on the new phone.

I logged something by mistake

Tap Undo on the message right after logging, or open the Jar tab and tap the entry in your history to remove it.

Check-ins aren’t arriving

Check that they’re on in the app (Settings › Check-ins) and that notifications are allowed for Quit Munchies in your iPhone’s Settings › Notifications. They’re skipped on days you’ve already logged an order.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Your data › Delete all my data erases everything on this phone. If you signed in with Apple, Settings › Account › Delete account also deletes your account and your cloud backup for good. See the Privacy Policy.
